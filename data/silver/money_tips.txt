10 Practical Money Tips

1. Track your spending weekly.
2. Save at least 10% of every paycheck.
3. Avoid impulse purchases by waiting 24 hours.
4. Build an emergency fund with 3–6 months of expenses.
5. Pay off high‑interest debt first.
6. Automate your savings.
7. Compare prices before buying anything expensive.
8. Invest consistently, even small amounts.
9. Review subscriptions every month.
10. Set clear financial goals with deadlines.
